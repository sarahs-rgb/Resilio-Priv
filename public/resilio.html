How these estimates are calculated

The battery continues to cover the full daily load. The selected mix divides daily Wh between solar and wind. Solar W = solar-share Wh ÷ peak-sun hours ÷ efficiency × margin. Wind rated W = wind-share Wh ÷ 24 hours ÷ capacity-factor assumption ÷ efficiency × margin. Capacity factor is average production divided by nameplate power; replace the app assumption with evidence from the exact turbine power curve and long-term hub-height wind data.

    Safety agreement · draft terms v2

    Understand the limits before using Resilio

    This draft needs review by a licensed attorney before public release. A disclaimer cannot remove legal duties or guarantee that Resilio is never liable.

    Resilio provides educational planning estimates and checklists, not emergency response, electrical engineering, medical advice, code approval, product certification, or installation services. Results depend on information entered by the user and may be incomplete or wrong.

    Resilio supports only stand-alone systems powering compatible plug-in devices through manufacturer-documented outputs. It does not design, instruct, or approve connections to household outlets, panels, wiring, or fixed circuits. Never rely on the app as the only source for a safety decision. Follow product manuals and qualified professionals. Do not work on energized equipment, bypass a battery-management system or protective device, or use damaged/unknown components.
